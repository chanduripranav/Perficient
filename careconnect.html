Business case for hospitals

How CareConnect pays off for a hospital

Hospitals already have to publish their prices. CareConnect turns that obligation into a patient acquisition channel: patients who can see and understand a price are more likely to book, the hospital is seen as transparent, and staff spend less time answering pricing calls.

The business problem

Business problem

A patient whose doctor orders an MRI does not know which hospital offers it, what it costs or what insurance covers. CMS requires every hospital to publish its prices, but they sit in large machine-readable files full of billing codes and different price types that patients cannot use.

So hospitals carry the cost of compliance without the benefit: price-shopping patients cannot compare them, and pricing questions go to the call center.

What CareConnect solves
  • For patients: plain-language answers about published prices, with the source for every number.
  • For hospitals: turns a required price file into a way to attract patients, show transparency and reduce pricing calls.
  • For Perficient: a reusable data, AI and digital experience accelerator to implement for hospital clients.

Compliance made useful

CMS requires hospitals to publish standard charges and present shoppable services in a consumer-friendly way. Hospitals with more than 550 beds can be fined $5,500 a day ($2,007,500 a year) for noncompliance.

How the privacy gateway helps Perficient

Proposed next step. The current proof of concept does not collect patient data.
1 · Faster approvals

Hospitals say yes faster

Hospital legal, compliance and security teams must approve any AI tool, and patient privacy (HIPAA) is their first question. A gateway that strips identifiers before the model sees them gives a clear answer: shorter sales cycles, fewer stalled deals.

2 · Bigger engagements

Unlocks the valuable roadmap

Today CareConnect uses only public price data. Out-of-pocket estimates, appointment booking and patient-specific navigation need personal context, and they cannot go live safely without a privacy layer.

3 · Reusable asset

Sold across every healthcare AI project

Chatbots, the AI Healthcare Concierge, call-center summaries and scheduling assistants all share this need. Built once, reused across clients: repeatable security, governance, integration and managed-services work.

4 · Trust and lower risk

Differentiation and protection

"Privacy by design" stands out against bolt-on chatbots, and it protects Perficient too: a patient-data leak damages the consultancy along with its client.
